The value case

Why the model is different

You don't need to wait until you can afford advice to start making progress. The diagnostic is free, with just a quick email verification (no password, ever), and every basic tool is free to use with no account needed at all. MoneyMatters+ is for going deeper once you're ready, not a gate you have to get through first.

This is what having it handled feels like.

Confidence isn't knowing everything. It's knowing where you stand, and having a plan you actually trust instead of a vague sense you should get around to it eventually.

Stop bracing for the next surprise.

A real emergency fund and a budget that reflects your actual life turn "what if something breaks" from a spiral into a line item you've already planned for.

Watch the number actually move.

Seeing your balance and payoff date update as you pay it down keeps debt from feeling like a fog you're stuck in. Progress you can see is progress you stick with.

Build the plan before they need it.

College costs, childcare, the first house down payment: the earlier you can see the shape of it, the less any single year has to carry.

Know why you spend, not just what you spent.

Seeing your patterns laid out plainly, not judged, just visible, is usually what breaks the cycle of guilt-spend-repeat that willpower alone never fixes.

Worth repeating: advisor matching is free either way, with or without MoneyMatters+. The $5/month is only ever for the toolset, never the price of talking to someone.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to join MoneyMatters+ to talk to an advisor?

No. Advisor matching is free whether or not you're on MoneyMatters+. It unlocks once you've taken your Financial Health Score, not once you've paid.

What if I just want the free tools?

Stay free as long as you like. The diagnostic and the Budget, Retirement, and Investment tools have no signup wall and never require a subscription.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, no lock-in. Cancel anytime and keep access through the end of your billing period.

What counts as a "new tool"?

Any calculator or feature we add to the native toolset after you join. It's included automatically, no extra charge and nothing to re-subscribe to.
