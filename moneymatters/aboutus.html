Questions

What's actually free?

The Financial Health Score and Net Worth diagnostics, and the Budget, Retirement, and Investment tools: all free, no signup wall to try them. Creating an account (via a magic-link email, no password) saves your results and unlocks the community and advisor matching.

What do I get for $5/month?

Advanced versions of the tools, new tools as we release them, explainer and how-to videos, webinar access, and advisor Q&A sessions. One subscription, everything included, no per-tool pricing.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, no lock-in. Cancel anytime and keep access through the end of your billing period.

Is this financial advice?

No. MoneyMatters tools are educational. For decisions with major consequences, connect with one of our vetted advisors or another licensed financial professional.

How do you actually vet advisors?

Every advisor in our network is confirmed CFP®-certified, fee-only (no commissions), and held to a fiduciary standard before they're allowed in front of any user. See where the money goes for the full picture.

How is this different from apps like Mint or YNAB?

Those are subscription apps built around linking your bank accounts and paywalled tiers. Ours run on numbers you enter yourself, every formula is visible, and going deeper (advanced tools or a real advisor) is your call, not a paywall you hit by accident.
