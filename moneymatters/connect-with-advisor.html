Advisor Connect

Every advisor in our network is fee-only, fiduciary, and vetted before they ever see your name. Matching is free — it unlocks once you've taken your Financial Health Score.

We ask a few questions about your situation, then match you with a vetted advisor who actually specializes in it — not just whoever's next in line.

How it works

1

Take your Financial Health Score

Free, under 2 minutes. This is what unlocks advisor matching — we don't ask for it separately.

2

Opt in and share your situation

Choose whether to connect, and optionally flag anything relevant — inheritance, a business sale, or anything else worth knowing.

3

Our team makes the match

We review your results and connect you with a vetted advisor suited to your situation — no cold calls, no sales pressure.

After you request a connection

1

You share a bit more context

The short intake form on your results page — what you need help with, and how specific to be about your location.

2

Our team reviews and matches

We pair your situation with an advisor who actually specializes in it.

3

The advisor shares their background first

You see who they are and how they work before anything else happens.

4

You decide — no pressure either way

Take the meeting or don't. There's no cost and no obligation, and you can stop at any point.

What "vetted" actually means

CFP®-certified, fee-only, fiduciary — every advisor, no exceptions.

CFP® certified.

Every advisor in our network holds a CERTIFIED FINANCIAL PLANNER™ certification — the industry's standard for competency and ethics.

Fee-only.

No commissions, no product kickbacks. Advisors are paid directly by their clients, not by which products they recommend.

Fiduciary duty.

Legally required to act in your best interest, full stop — not just "suitable" for you.
